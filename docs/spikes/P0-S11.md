## 1. Goal and pass criteria

From `roadmap.md` P0-S11 (`context_pack.md` §4) and the camera facts in §3.3: resolve **U11** —
measure open-to-first-frame delay after sleep / hibernate / lid / Fast Startup and tune the
backoff/re-enumeration policy; ensure the camera LED is off when idle and the armed window is
bounded to ≤ 8 s. Final acceptance is re-verified in P4 (`roadmap.md` §6 hardware acceptance matrix).

Pass conditions (all OWNER-run):
1. After **each** power transition (sleep/resume, hibernate/resume, lid close/open, Fast Startup
   boot, cold boot, Windows Update restart), the service can open the camera and read a **confirmed
   first frame** within the bounded deadline.
2. Open-to-first-frame delay is measured and the backoff (0/100/200/400/800 ms … to deadline) plus
   re-enumeration policy recovers within the **≤ 8 s armed window**.
3. The camera LED is **off when idle** and only on during the short armed window; it turns off again
   after each attempt / on cancel / on disable.
4. The camera is declared "ready" only after a real frame — not on `open()` success.
5. Normal password/PIN fallback remains intact after every power transition.

## 3. Offline-verified facts (from `context_pack.md` §3.3/§10 and the Wave-3 plan)

These are **(a) OFFLINE FACTS** — not runtime observations.

- **Cold-boot / resume delay [R15]:** prior art captures from a LocalSystem service with retries and
  a confirmed frame read; after cold boot or resume the camera can take **seconds** to become ready.
  This is why readiness is a spike, not an assumption — **VERIFY** on our hardware.
- **Backoff + first-frame confirmation (`context_pack.md` §3.3):** open with backoff
  (≈ 0, 100, 200, 400, 800 ms … until the deadline); declare "ready" only after a real frame is
  read; re-enumerate on failure. Discard the first N frames (default 5) for exposure/white-balance.
- **Exclusive ownership + LED (`context_pack.md` §3.3, §12):** the service owns the camera; the UI
  never opens it. During enrolment/calibration the wizard warns the user to close other camera apps
  to avoid contention. Keeping the camera closed when idle keeps the LED off (a safety/privacy
  property, derived from exclusive, short-lived opens).
- **First-unlock-after-boot rule (`context_pack.md` §3.7):** Microsoft KB 4073719 — custom providers
  may not load at the first logon after a restart when Windows auto-completes sign-in; workaround is
  `DisableAutomaticRestartSignOn`. Consistent with our "password after boot" policy.
- **Reference-laptop fact sheet (`context_pack.md` §10):** `winver`, `Get-Tpm`, `manage-bde -status`,
  `Confirm-SecureBootUEFI`, `Get-PnpDevice -Class Camera,Image`, `Win32_Processor`, camera VID/PID +
  formats, Smart App Control state, account type — recorded here for completeness (P0-S12).
- **Planned seam (Wave-3 plan):** `kenali_camera::mf::MfSource::open(&mut self, &CameraPin,
  deadline: Instant)` implements the backoff + first-frame confirmation; `enumerate_pins()` supports
  re-enumeration. The ≤ 8 s armed window and LED-off-when-idle are runtime properties measured here.

## 4. Procedure — exact manual VM / hardware steps (OWNER only)

These are **(b) OWNER MANUAL STEPS**, run by hand after the checkpoint. The agent runs none.

1. **Prepare the VM / reference laptop.** Checkpoint; record §2 rows; confirm sleep/hibernate/Fast
   Startup availability. Keep a second local admin with a password. Do all work in the VM first;
   final acceptance repeats on the reference laptop (P4).
2. **Build + install.** Build `kenali-svc` with `MfSource` (W3-06) for MSVC; install via the gated
   `xtask install --vm` (`KENALI_ALLOW_INSTALL=1`). Confirm the service is not holding the camera
   at idle.
3. **Baseline — cold boot.** Cold boot the VM; at the lock screen trigger an attempt and measure
   open-to-first-frame delay. Confirm a confirmed frame within the ≤ 8 s armed window and that the
   backoff timing matches 0/100/200/400/800 ms … to deadline. Record the delay and the LED behavior.
4. **Sleep / resume.** Put the VM to sleep (`powercfg /change` or Start → Sleep), wait, resume, lock,
   and trigger an attempt. Measure open-to-first-frame delay and recovery; repeat ≥ 3 times. Record
   each run and whether re-enumeration was needed.
5. **Hibernate / resume.** Enable/use hibernate, hibernate, resume, lock, trigger an attempt. Measure
   and record (≥ 3 runs).
6. **Lid close / open (reference laptop).** On the reference laptop, close the lid to sleep, open,
   lock, trigger an attempt. Measure and record (≥ 3 runs). If the VM cannot emulate a lid, mark
   **VERIFY / NOT RUN** and do this only on the laptop.
7. **Fast Startup.** Confirm Fast Startup state; shut down and power on (Fast Startup path), lock,
   trigger an attempt. Measure and record (≥ 3 runs).
8. **Windows Update restart.** Trigger/observe a restart that includes updates, lock, trigger an
   attempt. Confirm the KB 4073719 "first logon after restart" behavior and that password fallback
   works; measure readiness.
9. **LED-off-when-idle check.** With no attempt active, physically confirm the camera LED is off and
   no process holds a camera handle. Start an attempt: LED on only during the armed window; on
   match/deny/cancel/disable, LED off again and the handle released. Repeat after each power
   transition.
10. **Password fallback + no-retry.** After every transition, sign in with the password/PIN tile and
    confirm it works with no delay and is never hidden/filtered. Confirm a failure status disables
    face unlock and does not retry.
11. **Reset.** Leave the VM/laptop locked but idle, camera LED off, service healthy.

## 8. Decision

**Provisional: GO (proceed to OWNER-run VM / hardware verification).** The documented backoff +
first-frame-confirmation design is the right shape for power-transition readiness; nothing here
establishes that readiness is unsafe to attempt in a checkpointed VM.

This decision is **provisional** — no Windows runtime or hardware evidence exists on this host. It
becomes:
- **GO** if every transition yields a confirmed first frame within ≤ 8 s, the LED is off when idle,
  and password fallback works;
- **PIVOT** if a transition needs a longer armed window or a different re-enumeration policy — tune
  the backoff/deadline and re-measure (keep the LED-off-when-idle property);
- **STOP** if the camera cannot be reliably ready after common transitions or the LED cannot be made
  to turn off when idle — do not ship an armed camera that stays on.
