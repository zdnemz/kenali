## 1. Goal and pass criteria

From `roadmap.md` P0-S2 and `context_pack.md` §4: open the built-in RGB camera from a **SYSTEM
service in Session 0 at the lock screen** via Media Foundation `IMFSourceReader`, with backoff and
first-frame confirmation; decide **U1** (is Session-0 capture at the lock screen possible?) and
**U10** (is LocalSystem required, or does a virtual service account work for camera + TPM?).

Pass conditions (all OWNER-run in the VM):
1. A real frame is read from the camera by the service while the session is locked (Session 0),
   using `IMFSourceReader` — not merely an `open()` success.
2. Camera open succeeds with the documented backoff (0/100/200/400/800 ms … to the deadline) and
   the camera is declared ready **only after** a real frame is read.
3. Re-enumeration on failure recovers the camera (after a transient failure / unplug-replug).
4. First N frames (default 5) are discarded for auto-exposure / white-balance convergence.
5. The physical camera is pinned by symbolic link + hardware ID; a different camera is rejected
   (SEC-16).
6. DirectShow fallback works if Media Foundation is blocked in the service.
7. U10 answered: LocalSystem vs virtual service account (VSA) recorded for **both** camera and TPM.
8. Normal password/PIN fallback at the lock screen remains intact throughout.

## 3. Offline-verified facts (from `context_pack.md` §3.3, §4, §12 and the Wave-3 plan)

These are **(a) OFFLINE FACTS**, transcribed from the docs — not observations on this host.

- **Media Foundation, not WinRT `MediaCapture`:** WinRT `MediaCapture` inside a service has been
  reported to throw access-denied [R35]. Use Media Foundation (`IMFSourceReader`) with DirectShow
  as the fallback if MF is blocked. Do not use `nokhwa` in production [R34].
- **Session-0 prior art:** prior art captures from a LocalSystem service (DirectShow) with retries
  and a confirmed frame read; after cold boot or resume the camera can take seconds to become ready
  [R15]. This is prior art, not proof for our VM — U1 is **VERIFY**.
- **Backoff + first-frame confirmation:** open with backoff (≈ 0, 100, 200, 400, 800 ms … until the
  deadline); declare the camera "ready" only after a real frame is read; re-enumerate on failure.
- **Warm-up frames:** discard the first N frames (default 5) for auto-exposure / white-balance
  convergence; tune per camera.
- **Camera pinning:** pin the physical camera at enrolment (symbolic link + hardware ID); reject
  anything else (SEC-16). The UI never opens the camera (`context_pack.md` §2.17, §12).
- **Safe seam (Wave-3 plan):** `kenali_camera::mf::MfSource` implements the merged
  `kenali_core::frame::FrameSource` trait (`open(&mut self, &CameraPin, Instant)`,
  `next_frame(&mut self, Instant) -> Result<Frame, CaptureError>`, `close`, `Send`). Pin helpers:
  `kenali_camera::pin::{enumerate_pins, verify_pin}`. `MfSource`, `FileSource` (replay) and
  `SyntheticSource` are re-exported from `kenali_camera::lib.rs`.
- **Account question (U10):** LocalSystem vs a virtual service account (VSA) for both camera and
  TPM is unresolved offline — **VERIFY**. (The auth-pipe SDDL `D:P(A;;GA;;;SY)` in
  `context_pack.md` §9 assumes SYSTEM; a VSA would change that contract.)
- **NOT in scope:** no ML, no TPM unwrap (P0-S4), no auth pipe (P0-S3), no readiness-after-resume
  matrix (P0-S11) in this spike.

## 4. Procedure — exact manual VM steps (OWNER only)

These are **(b) OWNER MANUAL STEPS**. The OWNER runs them by hand in the VM, after the checkpoint
from `dev_safety_runbook.md` §1/§2 is in place. The agent runs none of them.

1. **Prepare the VM.** Take a checkpoint. Confirm the §2 rows are recorded. Keep a second local
   admin account with a working password. Do all work in the **VM**, not the physical host's main
   account.
2. **Build the camera crate** for `x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` from the versioned checkout (W3-06
   `crates/kenali-camera/src/mf.rs`, `MfSource`). This is type-checked on the host; no camera is
   opened here.
3. **Install the service under LocalSystem first.** In an elevated PowerShell in the VM, set the
   gate and install (`cargo xtask install --vm`, gated by `KENALI_ALLOW_INSTALL=1` per
   `context_pack.md` §10). Confirm `sc qc KenaliSvc` shows `SERVICE_ACCOUNT = LocalSystem`.
4. **Test U1 — Session-0 capture at the lock screen.** Lock the session (`Win+L`) so the service is
   running in Session 0 while LogonUI is showing. Trigger the camera-open path. Confirm via service
   trace/Event Log that `IMFSourceReader` opens the camera and that a **real frame** is read and
   confirmed — not just a successful `open()`. Record the open-to-first-frame delay and the session
   id in §5.
5. **Test the backoff.** Force a transient failure (e.g. hold the camera with another app, or start
   the service before the camera is available) and confirm the open retries at 0/100/200/400/800 ms
   … to the deadline and does not spin or block unbounded. Confirm "ready" is set only after a real
   frame.
6. **Test re-enumeration.** While the service is mid-cycle, unplug/replug the camera (or disable/
   re-enable it in Device Manager). Confirm `enumerate_pins()` re-enumerates and the service
   recovers within the deadline.
7. **Test warm-up frames.** Confirm the first 5 frames are discarded and the kept frames look
   converged (no severe under/over-exposure). Tune N per this camera if needed; record the value.
8. **Test camera pinning (SEC-16).** Confirm the enrolled pin (symbolic link + hardware ID) is
   accepted and a second / different camera is rejected. If a second camera is unavailable, document
   that in §6 as **VERIFY**.
9. **Test the DirectShow fallback.** If Media Foundation is blocked in the service, confirm the
   DirectShow fallback captures a confirmed frame. If MF works, record "fallback not exercised".
10. **Test U10 — virtual service account.** Change `KenaliSvc` to a virtual service account (e.g.
    `NT SERVICE\KenaliSvc`) in the VM and repeat steps 4–8 for the camera. Then repeat the TPM
    open/wrap path from P0-S4 under the VSA (read-only — do not change key scope irreversibly).
    Record whether both camera and TPM work under the VSA.
11. **Confirm fallback intact.** At every step, use the normal password/PIN tile to sign in; it must
    work with no delay and must not be hidden or filtered.
12. **Reset.** Restore the service account to the chosen final value and re-run a single confirmed
    capture to leave the VM in a known-good state.
